Who we are

A student-built idea born from a simple question: what if we made the lesser-discovered India easier to discover?

We believe the next great travel story is already here.

OOR began with a simple observation: Chhattisgarh has extraordinary places, cultures, crafts and stories — but many of them remain outside the usual tourism map.

We asked ourselves: what if discovering these places could be easier for travellers, while creating more opportunity for the local people who make these experiences special? That question became OOR.

We're building a platform that connects travellers with lesser-explored destinations, local guides, cultural experiences and authentic handicrafts — starting with Chhattisgarh, and eventually expanding across India.

— Built by students who believe tourism should help people discover more, and communities grow with it.

  • 01

    Discover beyond the usual

    Explore lesser-known destinations, local cultures, food, art, nature and experiences across Chhattisgarh.

  • 02

    Local connections

    We work with local guides, tourism operators, hosts, artisans and community partners who know their places best.

  • 03

    Experiences, not itineraries

    Small-group, meaningful experiences designed around people, stories, culture and place — not just a checklist of attractions.

  • 04

    Value stays local

    Through experiences and our artisan marketplace, OOR aims to connect more tourism spending with the local businesses, guides and craftspeople behind the experience.
